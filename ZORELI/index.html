FAQ / Frequently Asked Questions

Does Zoreli use OCR?

No — it uses a modern vision-language model for higher accuracy on real-world cards.

Where does enrichment data come from?

Only publicly available sources on the web.

Is my data private?

You control your data; delete cards and results anytime in the app.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone (iOS). iPad support planned.

Can I export to my CRM?

Yes — export CSV/Excel and import to your CRM.

Is this compliant for outreach?

Zoreli provides suggestions; you remain responsible for legal and policy compliance.
